# flameshot-safe

> Universal, zero-interruption screenshot launcher with Flameshot GUI and Maim fallback.

- Launch interactive Flameshot GUI (draw, annotate, select area):
  `flameshot-safe.sh gui`

- Capture fullscreen immediately and save to Pictures:
  `flameshot-safe.sh full`

- Capture active screen output:
  `flameshot-safe.sh screen`

- Capture with countdown delay in seconds:
  `flameshot-safe.sh delay {{5}}`

- Standard wrapper shorthand:
  `screenshot.sh {{gui|full|screen|delay}}`
