# agy

> Official Google Antigravity Agentic Coding Assistant CLI companion.

- Start an interactive Antigravity agentic session in the current directory:
  agy

- Resume and continue your most recent conversation directly in terminal:
  agy-last

- Ask a quick one-off question without entering full interactive mode:
  agy-ask "{{question or prompt}}"

- List available AI model tiers and agents:
  agy models
  agy agents
