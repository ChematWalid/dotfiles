# ripdrag

> Drag and drop files to and from the terminal using an ephemeral floating window.
> Wrapped by `drop` utility.

- Open drag-and-drop window for specific files:
  `ripdrag <file1> <file2>`

- Drag and exit immediately on successful drop:
  `ripdrag -x -a <file>`

- Quick drop wrapper:
  `drop <file>`
