# ncspot

> Cross-platform ncurses Spotify client written in Rust with MPRIS support.
> Configured with Catppuccin Mocha theme in ~/.config/ncspot/config.toml.

- Start ncspot:
  `ncspot`

- Search for tracks, albums, or artists:
  Press `/` within ncspot

- Play/pause current track:
  Press `Space`

- View queue:
  Press `q`
