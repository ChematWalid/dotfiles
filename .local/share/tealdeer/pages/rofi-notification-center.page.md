# rofi-notification-center

> Interactive Notification Center for Dunst & Rofi with Catppuccin Mocha theme.
> Displays history, allows focusing source applications, and clearing notifications.

- Open interactive notification history list:
  `rofi-notification-center.sh`

- Close all active notifications:
  `dunstctl close-all`

- Redisplay last notification:
  `dunstctl history-pop`
